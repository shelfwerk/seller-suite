# Downloading the search term report

Give the user these steps in their language. Menu names differ slightly between Seller Central
and the Amazon Ads console and change from time to time; describe the path, not exact buttons.

## English

1. Open **Seller Central → Advertising → Campaign Manager** (or advertising.amazon.com) and go to
   **Measurement & Reporting → Sponsored ads reports** (older menus: **Reports**).
2. Create a report: Ad product **Sponsored Products**, report category **Search term**, time unit
   **Summary**, period **last 30 or 60 days** (the report reaches back about 65 days).
3. Run it, download the **.xlsx** (or .csv) and give TermSweep the file — attach it in Claude or
   save it in the working folder.

## Deutsch

1. **Seller Central → Werbung → Kampagnenmanager** (oder advertising.amazon.de) öffnen und zu
   **Messung und Berichte → Berichte zu gesponserten Anzeigen** gehen (ältere Menüs: **Berichte**).
2. Bericht erstellen: Werbeprodukt **Sponsored Products**, Berichtskategorie **Suchbegriff**,
   Zeiteinheit **Zusammenfassung**, Zeitraum **letzte 30 oder 60 Tage** (der Bericht reicht ca.
   65 Tage zurück).
3. Ausführen, die **.xlsx** (oder .csv) herunterladen und TermSweep geben — im Chat anhängen oder
   in den Arbeitsordner legen.

## Accepted files

- `.xlsx`, `.csv`, `.tsv`, `.txt`; summary or daily; any console language
  (EN/DE/FR/IT/ES/NL/SE/PL and others when the headers are recognisable).
- The old and the 2026 column names (`7 Day Total Sales` / `Sales`, `7 Day Total Orders (#)` /
  `Purchases (#)`, German `Umsatz…`/`Verkäufe`, `Bestellungen…`/`Käufe`).
- The *SP Search Term Report* tab inside a bulk download (it carries the IDs TermSweep Pro needs).
- Not supported: the old `.xls` format — download `.xlsx` instead.
