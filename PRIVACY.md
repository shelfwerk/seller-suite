
# Privacy: Shelfwerk Seller Suite (free plugins)

**Short version:** the plugins run on your computer, send nothing to us, and have no telemetry.

- **Local processing.** The bundled Python scripts read the report files you give them and write results into your working folder.
  They don't log in anywhere, don't call any API and don't send data to us or to third parties.
- **One network request, only on demand.** If you give ListingDoctor ASINs instead of a file, it fetches the public Amazon product page
  (`https://<marketplace>/dp/<ASIN>`) from your computer, without a login and without personal data.
- **Claude.** The plugins run inside Claude. Whatever you share in the chat (for example a report summary) is processed by Claude like any
  other file, under the terms of your Claude plan with Anthropic. We have no access to your conversations.
- **No accounts, no cookies, no tracking** in the plugins.
- **Support emails** you send us are used only to answer you and are deleted when no longer needed.
